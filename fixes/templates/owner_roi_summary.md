## The Tip Credit You're Probably Not Claiming

Your 3 tipped employees (2 servers + 1 bartender) earn ~$43,200/year in tips.
You pay employer payroll taxes on those tips (~$5,842/year).

**Quebec gives you 75% of that back: $4,382 cash refund.**

This is a **refundable credit** — you get it even if Giwa has zero profit.
You get it even in a loss year. It's a cheque from Revenu Québec.

Most small restaurants don't claim this. Your CPA will handle it.

## The Receipt Problem

Every receipt you lose costs you ~15% in tax recovery:
- A $200 Sysco invoice without a receipt = $29.95 in lost GST/QST claims
- Lose 5 receipts per week × 52 weeks = **$7,787/year gone**

**Fix:** The Dext app on your phone. Snap a photo, it's done.
For your big suppliers (Sysco, SAQ), invoices arrive by email —
the system grabs them automatically. You don't touch anything.

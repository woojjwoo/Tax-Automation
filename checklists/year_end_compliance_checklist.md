# Year-End Compliance Checklist — Giwa Restaurant (Montreal)

**Client:** Giwa Restaurant Inc.
**Jurisdiction:** Federal (CRA) + Quebec (Revenu Québec)
**Industry:** Hospitality / Food Services (NAICS 7225)
**Filings:** T2 Corporation Income Tax Return, CO-17 Déclaration de revenus des sociétés

---

## Phase 1: Pre-Close Procedures (60–90 Days Before Year-End)

### 1.1 General Ledger & Chart of Accounts Review

- [ ] Confirm chart of accounts aligns with ASPE (Part II of the CPA Canada Handbook)
  or ASNPO if applicable
- [ ] Review sub-ledger to GL reconciliations (AP, AR, inventory, fixed assets)
- [ ] Verify intercompany balances are eliminated (if Giwa has related entities)
- [ ] Reconcile all bank accounts to GL — ensure outstanding items are less than 30 days
- [ ] Review suspense / clearing accounts — zero balances required at year-end
- [ ] Confirm foreign currency transactions are translated at year-end rate (IAS 21 / S.261 ITA)

### 1.2 Revenue Recognition — Restaurant-Specific

- [ ] Reconcile POS system daily Z-tapes to GL revenue accounts
- [ ] **SRM/MEV reconciliation** — see dedicated checklist (`srm_mev_reconciliation.md`)
- [ ] Verify gift card liabilities (deferred revenue) per ASPE 3400
- [ ] Reconcile Uber Eats / DoorDash / Skip the Dishes commissions and net revenue
- [ ] Verify catering deposits recorded as deferred revenue until event date
- [ ] Confirm revenue split: dine-in vs. takeout vs. delivery (QST meal implications)

### 1.3 Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

- [ ] Perform physical inventory count or perpetual inventory reconciliation
- [ ] Verify food cost percentage is within industry norms (28–35%)
- [ ] Verify beverage cost percentage (alcohol: 18–24%, non-alcohol: 10–15%)
- [ ] Review waste and spoilage write-offs — ensure proper documentation
- [ ] Reconcile supplier statements to AP sub-ledger

### 1.4 Payroll Pre-Close

- [ ] Reconcile payroll journal entries to payroll provider reports
- [ ] Verify all tip income is captured per SRM/MEV data
- [ ] Confirm employer/employee CPP, QPP, EI, QPIP source deductions are current
- [ ] Review CNESST (workers' compensation) classification — code 91030 for restaurants
- [ ] Verify Normes du travail compliance (minimum wage, hours, statutory holidays)
- [ ] Prepare preliminary T4 / RL-1 data for accuracy

---

## Phase 2: Year-End Adjusting Entries

### 2.1 Accruals & Provisions

- [ ] Accrue unpaid wages, vacation pay (4% or 6% per QC Normes du travail)
- [ ] Accrue employer payroll taxes (CPP/QPP, EI, QPIP, CNESST, health services fund)
- [ ] Accrue property tax / municipal tax if paid semi-annually
- [ ] Accrue utilities (Hydro-Québec, gas) spanning year-end
- [ ] Accrue professional fees (audit, legal, tax return preparation)
- [ ] Review and accrue lease obligations (ASPE 3065 or IFRS 16 if applicable)
- [ ] Accrue GST/QST payable/receivable — ensure returns filed are reconciled

### 2.2 Capital Assets & Amortization

- [ ] Update fixed asset register — additions, dispositions, impairment
- [ ] Calculate CCA per prescribed classes:
  - Class 8 (20%) — kitchen equipment, furniture, fixtures
  - Class 10 (30%) — delivery vehicles
  - Class 12 (100%) — smallwares under $500 (cutlery, dishes, utensils)
  - Class 13 — leasehold improvements (over lease term + one renewal)
  - Class 50 (55%) — computer hardware (POS terminals)
  - Class 14.1 (5%) — goodwill, liquor licence (post-2017)
- [ ] Verify half-year rule (Reg. 1100(2)) applied to current-year acquisitions
- [ ] Confirm Accelerated Investment Incentive Property (AIIP) eligibility if applicable
- [ ] Reconcile accounting amortization vs. tax CCA — note timing differences

### 2.3 Tax-Specific Adjustments

- [ ] Calculate meals & entertainment limitation (50% per ITA S.67.1)
  - Note: 100% deductible if provided to all employees as a taxable benefit
- [ ] Review automobile / delivery vehicle expenses — logbook requirement
- [ ] Verify home-office deduction if owner uses home for admin (T2200 / TP-64.3-V)
- [ ] Identify and quantify permanent vs. temporary differences for tax provision
- [ ] Calculate Quebec SBD (Small Business Deduction) — verify CCPC status
- [ ] Verify active business income threshold ($500K federal / $500K QC for reduced rate)

---

## Phase 3: Federal T2 Filing

### 3.1 T2 Return Preparation

- [ ] Complete GIFI-coded financial statements (S.100 to S.141)
- [ ] Schedule 1: Net Income (Loss) for Income Tax Purposes — reconciliation
- [ ] Schedule 8: CCA calculations
- [ ] Schedule 50: Shareholder Information (if privately held)
- [ ] Schedule 125: Income Statement Information (GIFI)
- [ ] Schedule 100: Balance Sheet Information (GIFI)
- [ ] Schedule 200: T2 Corporate Tax Return
- [ ] Schedule 3: Dividends Received, Taxable Dividends Paid
- [ ] Schedule 4: Corporation Loss Continuity and Application
- [ ] Calculate Part I federal tax and SBD (S.125 ITA)
- [ ] Apply any available SR&ED credits (if applicable to food tech R&D)
- [ ] Verify instalment payments made — reconcile to My Business Account
- [ ] File electronically via certified CRA software (mandatory for corporations)

### 3.2 Federal Tax Rates (for Reference)

| Category | Rate |
|----------|------|
| General corporate rate | 15% (38% - 10% abatement - 13% general reduction) |
| Small business rate (CCPC, ABI ≤ $500K) | 9% |
| Refundable tax on investment income | Part IV / RDTOH |

## Phase 4: Quebec CO-17 Filing

### 4.1 CO-17 Return Preparation

- [ ] Complete CO-17 base return — import from T2 with Quebec adjustments
- [ ] CO-17.A.1: Net income for income tax purposes (Quebec adjustments)
- [ ] Schedule of CCA for Quebec — note differences from federal classes if any
- [ ] Calculate Quebec small business deduction (3.2% on first $500K of ABI)
- [ ] Verify QC Health Services Fund (HSF) contribution — rate based on payroll
- [ ] Calculate QC compensatory tax on financial institutions (if applicable — N/A for restaurant)
- [ ] Apply Quebec tax credits:
  - [ ] **PME-6.1: Tax Credit for Reporting of Tips** — see `tip_credit_pme_6_1.md`
  - [ ] Credit for on-the-job training (if apprentice program)
  - [ ] SME growth prescription (déduction PME if applicable)
- [ ] Reconcile QST returns filed vs. QST per books
- [ ] Verify instalment payments to Revenu Québec — reconcile to Mon Dossier
- [ ] File electronically via certified Revenu Québec software

### 4.2 Quebec Tax Rates (for Reference)

| Category | Rate |
|----------|------|
| General corporate rate | 11.5% |
| Small business rate (CCPC, ABI ≤ $500K) | 3.2% |
| Health Services Fund (payroll < $1M) | 1.65% |
| CNESST (restaurant classification) | Varies by experience rating |

## Phase 5: Indirect Tax Compliance (GST/QST)

- [ ] Reconcile GST collected vs. GST reported on returns (RT account)
- [ ] Reconcile QST collected vs. QST reported on returns (QC account)
- [ ] Verify ITCs (Input Tax Credits) claimed are supported by invoices
- [ ] Verify ITRs (Input Tax Refunds) for QST are properly claimed
- [ ] Confirm meal surcharges and service charges subject to GST/QST
- [ ] Verify tip-out amounts are not subject to GST/QST (not consideration for supply)
- [ ] Reconcile SRM/MEV tax data to GST/QST returns
- [ ] Ensure Quick Method election (if applicable) is documented
- [ ] File any outstanding GST/QST returns before T2/CO-17 filing

---

## Phase 6: Information Returns & Slips

- [ ] Prepare and file T4 slips (employment income) — deadline: last day of February
- [ ] Prepare and file RL-1 slips (Quebec equivalent) — deadline: last day of February
- [ ] Report allocated tips on T4/RL-1 if direct tips are below SRM/MEV threshold
- [ ] Prepare T4A / RL-1 for subcontractors if applicable (DJs, musicians, etc.)
- [ ] File T5 / RL-3 if dividends paid to shareholders
- [ ] Prepare T2 Summary and Quebec RL-1 Summary
- [ ] Verify CPP/QPP, EI, QPIP maximums per employee
